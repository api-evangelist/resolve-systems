# Resolve Systems

> Resolve Systems (resolve.io) is an enterprise IT automation and AIOps platform whose "Agentic Resolution Fabric" combines a Knowledge Agent, an Automation Agent, and Agent Assist to automate incident resolution, service-desk workflows, and cross-domain IT remediation. Product lines include Resolve Actions, Actions Pro, Actions Express, and Barista. Resolve is a portfolio company of Insight Partners. Resolve does not appear to publish a public, self-service developer API, OpenAPI definition, or open developer portal; integration and automation are delivered through platform connectors and customer-gated documentation.
